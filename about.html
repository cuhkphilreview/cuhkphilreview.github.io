How we work

CUHK Philosophy Review is a student-run interview journal. Each issue records conversations with members of the Department of Philosophy about their work, their paths into philosophy and what they hope the Department becomes.

Preparation

Before drafting questions, the editors read the interviewee’s published work. The questions begin from what the interviewee takes to be central. A second editor reviews every set of questions before it is sent.

Approval

Every interviewee sees the written text and approves it before publication. Recordings stay private unless the interviewee agrees otherwise. Corrections are recorded at the foot of the article concerned.

Editors

Chuk Tai Wai and Lee Kwok Shun, co-founders, with two student editors who joined for the first issue. A first-year editor is in training to take over the journal when the founders graduate.
