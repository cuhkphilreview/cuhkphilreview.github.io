How we work

CUHK Philosophy Review is a student-run interview journal. Each issue records conversations with members of the Department of Philosophy about their work, their paths into philosophy and what they hope the Department becomes.

Preparation

Before drafting questions, the editors read the interviewee’s published work. The questions begin from what the interviewee takes to be central. A second editor reviews every set of questions before it is sent.

Approval

Every interviewee sees the written text and approves it before publication. Recordings stay private unless the interviewee agrees otherwise. Corrections are recorded at the foot of the article concerned.

Editors

The three editors are Chuk Tai Wai (Wisdom, 祝大為), Lee Kwok Shun (Shun, 李國舜) and Lee Pak Yi (Peggy, 李柏怡). Wisdom and Shun co-founded the journal. Peggy joined later and is a second-year student. All three share editorial decisions.

Future editors

We are getting to know first-year students, including those we met at the Orientation Camp, and considering who could carry the journal forward. We look for sustained interest in philosophy, sound judgement and the ability to finish work.

We plan to allow at least one semester to assess candidates before inviting a new editor. A successor has yet to be appointed. Training will begin after a suitable candidate joins the team.

Faculty adviser

Professor Franz Mang, Department of Philosophy, The Chinese University of Hong Kong.

Publisher

Published through the philosophy student society of The Chinese University of Hong Kong (in formation). The editors are consulting the Department of Philosophy on the journal’s affiliation.
