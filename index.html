Women in Philosophy

An interview with Professor Ye Ru. She talks about how she chooses her questions, what she has learned about higher-order evidence, being one of the few women in formal epistemology, and what she would say to a philosopher just entering the field.

About the Review

A record of the Department, in its own voices

Students arrive in a department and rarely learn how the people teaching them arrived there. The routes taken, the questions abandoned, the reasons for staying. The CUHK Review of Philosophy puts those conversations on the record, one issue at a time, so that future students and colleagues can read them.
