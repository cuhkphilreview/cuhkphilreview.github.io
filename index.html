About the Review

A record of the Department, in its own voices

Students arrive in a department and rarely learn how the people teaching them arrived there. The routes taken, the questions abandoned, the reasons for staying. CUHK Philosophy Review puts those conversations on the record, one issue at a time, so that future students and colleagues can read them.
