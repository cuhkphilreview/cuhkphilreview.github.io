Contribute

Write for the Review, or be interviewed

The Review is edited by students and depends on the generosity of the people it interviews.

Students

Editors are admitted by trial: reading an interviewee’s work, drafting questions and editing a transcript with support from the current editors. Write to the editors with a short note on what you have read and what you would like to work on.

Members of the Department

If you would be willing to be interviewed for a future issue, or would like to suggest a theme, the editors would be glad to hear from you. Interviews take about an hour, and you approve the written text before it appears.

Letters

The Review is built as a living archive, and it relies on the voices of our community. If you have a perspective to share, a scholar you would like to see interviewed, or feedback on our inaugural issue, we welcome your letters.
